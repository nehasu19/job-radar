# Job boards by market

Pick the rows for every target market in the profile. Always add the **Global / remote** rows when any location is remote. Search boards through `WebSearch` (e.g. `site:` queries or the board name plus role and city), then confirm each posting on the employer's own careers page where possible. Boards change; if one stops returning results, note it in the report and move on.

LinkedIn, Indeed and Glassdoor exist in most markets. LinkedIn pages are not read directly (see SKILL.md step 4); Indeed and Glassdoor listings are fine to find roles, but confirm on the employer's page.

## Writing local-language queries

- Use the job titles local postings use, not literal translations (e.g. German "Softwaretester", "Testautomatisierer"; French "Ingénieur QA", "Ingénieur test et validation"; Spanish "Ingeniero de QA"; Dutch "Testautomatiseerder").
- Add the local gender marker where postings use one ("(m/w/d)" in DE/AT/CH, "(h/f)" in FR) to narrow to real postings.
- Search remote roles with the country name ("remote Germany", "télétravail France", "remoto España").
