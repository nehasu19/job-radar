What it does

Your job search, done while you sleep

Finds live jobs

Searches the main job boards for your countries, in English and the local language, and opens every posting to check it is still open.

Scores them honestly

Rates each job 0–100 against your resume: role, skills, location, languages, work rights and salary.

Keeps one tracker

Saves the good matches to a private page with no duplicates, and flags postings that have been taken down.

Drafts your documents

Writes a tailored resume and cover letter for the best matches, using only facts from your real resume.

Start in about 10 minutes

How to use it in Claude

You need a Claude account where Skills are available. If you use Claude through work and can't see Skills, ask your admin to turn them on.

  1. Download the skill

    Open the latest release on GitHub and download job-radar.skill. For better resumes, also download tailored-cv.skill and no-ai-slop.skill. To score your resume before you apply, add resume-check.skill.

  2. Add it to Claude

    In Claude, open Settings → Capabilities → Skills, choose to upload a skill, and select the file. Repeat for the other files. Menu names can change slightly between app versions.

  3. Start a new chat and ask for setup

    Attach your resume (PDF or Word) and send:

    Set up Job Radar for me

  4. Answer a few questions

    Claude asks for the roles you want, where you want to work, your languages, where you can work without a visa, your salary expectation and when you can start. Then it creates your own tracker page.

  5. Run your first search

    Run job radar

    New matches appear in your tracker with a score, the languages they need and why they fit.

  6. Make it daily (optional)

    Create a scheduled task that runs Job Radar every day at 8:45

    You get a phone notification only when something new turns up.

Tip: keep the tracker link somewhere handy. That page is where you mark jobs as shortlisted, applied or interviewing, and copy your details into application forms.

Built-in rules

What it will never do

Apply for you

No applications, no form submissions, no messages to employers or recruiters. You decide and you send.

Invent experience

Tailored resumes and resume-check rewrites only reorder and reword your real resume. No made-up skills, titles, dates, degrees or numbers.

Share your data

Your profile and jobs live in your own private tracker. To share Job Radar, share the skill, never your tracker.

Guess about visas or pay

Jobs you can't legally take are skipped. Salaries in another currency are noted, never converted.

Questions

Before you start

Is it free?

The skill is free and open source under the MIT licence. You need a Claude plan where Skills are available, and web search turned on.

I can't find Skills in my settings

Skills may be switched off for your account or organisation. If you use Claude through work or school, ask your admin to enable them. On a personal account, check that you're on a plan that includes Skills.

Do I need to be technical?

No. After uploading the file, everything happens in a normal chat. You never touch code.

Does it read LinkedIn?

No, LinkedIn blocks automated reading. You can set up LinkedIn job alerts by email, connect your email to Claude, and ask Job Radar to check those jobs on each company's own careers page.

Can my friend use my tracker?

No. The tracker holds your resume and contact details. Your friend downloads the skill and says "Set up Job Radar for me" to get their own.

Is the resume score what employers see?

No. It's a checklist score from 39 checks, not the number an employer's system gives. There is no universal ATS score, and most systems rank and filter resumes for a recruiter rather than reject them automatically. The score shows how much of the checklist your resume meets and which fixes help most.

What if a score looks wrong?

Each job explains its score and lists the gaps. Tell Claude what to change, for example "skip roles that need German above B1", and ask it to update your profile before the next search.
