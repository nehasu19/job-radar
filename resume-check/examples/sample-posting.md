You'll lead a team of 5 SDETs testing our UPI and card payments apps, and own how we test them.

- Test strategy for payments products
- Leading and mentoring SDETs; working with product and engineering leads

**Nice to have**
- Performance testing (JMeter or k6)
- Docker
- SQL for data checks
- ISTQB certification

**Education:** B.E./B.Tech in Computer Science or a related field.
